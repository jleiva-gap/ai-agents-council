Review mode: evaluate implementation against the ticket, acceptance criteria, codebase evidence, and risk posture.

Output requirements:
- Lead with a readiness verdict: Ready / Needs Work / Blocked
- For each acceptance criterion: state covered, partial, or missing with evidence
- Group findings by severity: blocking vs. non-blocking
- Tie every finding to a specific file, function, or artifact where possible
- End with a confidence score (High / Medium / Low) and the evidence basis for it
