Spike mode: frame uncertainty, experiments, hypotheses, and the learning needed before commitment.

Output requirements:
- Lead with the uncertainty being investigated and why it blocks confident planning
- State at least two competing hypotheses with the evidence that would confirm or refute each
- Describe the minimum viable experiment that resolves the uncertainty
- Set a time-box and define what done means for the spike
- End with decision criteria: what outcome recommends which path forward
